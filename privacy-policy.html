Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 24, 2026

At Edwin's Picks, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you visit our website or use our services, including sign-in through a third-party authentication provider.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: When you interact with our site or sign in through an authentication provider, we may collect your name, email address, or profile information (such as a username or account ID) that you explicitly allow on that provider's consent screen.
  • Non-Personal Information: We collect usage data such as IP addresses, browser type, device information, and website interactions (for example, pages visited and links clicked) via cookies or analytics tools.
  • Affiliate Data: When you click affiliate links, we may collect anonymized data about your clicks or purchases to track commissions, as permitted by our affiliate partners.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide the Service, including personalized product recommendations and features you request.
  • Authenticate you when you sign in through a third-party provider, and operate the permissions you approve.
  • Facilitate affiliate marketing, such as tracking clicks or purchases through affiliate links.
  • Improve our website's functionality and user experience through analytics.
  • Communicate with you, if you opt in, about updates or promotions.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service Providers: Third parties that help us operate the Service, such as authentication providers, hosting providers, and analytics providers.
  • Affiliate Partners: Anonymized data may be shared with affiliate programs to track referrals.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by law, we may disclose information to comply with legal obligations.

4. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience and track affiliate link activity. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

5. Third-Party Sign-In

If you sign in with an authentication provider, we access only the account information you explicitly allow, such as basic profile details. We store that data securely and use it only to operate sign-in and the features you request. Our use of that information follows this Privacy Policy and the user-data policies of the provider you chose. You can review and revoke our access at any time in that provider's account or security settings. Revoking access may limit features that depend on that sign-in.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, update, or delete your personal information.
  • Opt out of data collection, for example by disabling cookies or revoking an authentication provider's app permissions.
  • Contact us at privacy@edwinslifestylepicks.com for data-related requests.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard measures to protect your data, but no system is completely secure. We encourage you to use strong passwords and secure connections.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at privacy@edwinslifestylepicks.com.
